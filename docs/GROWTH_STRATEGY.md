## Put it all together

1. **Define how-to content** from empowerment pillars (boundaries, toxic dynamics, confidence, positive energy, constructive jealousy, toxic family).
2. **Generate** with ChatGPT/OpenAI (Saphira `openai_content`).
3. **Schedule** with Publer + FeedHive.
4. **Grow followers organically** — consistency, SEO/ASO bios, real engagement. No bots or bought followers.
5. **Analyze** what works; adjust series and CTAs.
6. **Monetize** with honest funnels (guides, email, community) — no manufactured outrage.

## SEO / ASO

- Niche keywords in bio and series titles
- Clear value in first line of captions
- Accessible text on images/video
